**How the money is worked out.**
- **Exchange rates:** ECB reference rates of 2026-11-19: 1 EUR = 369.43 HUF = 1.0992 USD = 0.83581 GBP.
- **VAT:** a private buyer in Hungary pays 27 % VAT everywhere.
  - Hungarian shops include it in their gross price. Hestore shows net prices by default ("ÁFA nélkül").
  - EU shops charge it at checkout. Kiwi, Opencircuit, RobotShop EU and Amazon.de show their home-country VAT or net prices; 27 % is applied.
  - AliExpress collects it at checkout.
- **VAT-exempt shop:** Makerlogic3D charges no VAT, so its price is final and the two columns match.
- **Columns:** "EUR net" is the price without any VAT; "EUR incl. VAT" is what you would pay.
- **Purchase units:** every line is priced by what you actually have to buy (whole spools, magnet tens, kits, metres, sheets), not pro-rated.

**How sure each line is.** Every link was opened on 2026-11-19. Two caveats:
- **Unverified ("listing"):** AliExpress shows a captcha to the server that checked the links, so most AliExpress lines (except the motors) are priced from the search listing. Open those pages and re-check the variant before ordering. They are marked "listing" in the Stock column.
- **Shipping estimates:** where a shop shows shipping only in the cart, the line says "est.".

## Open decisions for Adam (spending calls; defaults are in the totals)

1. **Building the 3S4P pack.**
   - **Default:** spot-weld it with pure nickel strip (P3) and fish-paper rings (P4), about EUR 9 incl. VAT, plus a spot welder if you don't have one (not priced here).
   - **Alternatives:** buy a ready-made 3S4P pack that fits the 38 x 58 x 134 mm block, or have a battery shop build one from the 12 cells (P1, EUR 41). Neither was priced. Either would replace P1-P4 (about EUR 52 incl. VAT).
   - **Don't** solder onto the cells.
2. **LD19 source.**
   - **Default:** RobotShop EU, in stock and verified: EUR 137 incl. VAT (+ shared RobotShop shipping).
   - **Alternative:** AliExpress LDROBOT listing at EUR 75-81 incl. VAT, about **EUR 56-62 less**. The variant (LD19 vs LD06/LD14) and delivery to Hungary are unverified.
3. **Wheels source.**
   - **Default:** the Nexus 100 x 50 mm set the model is drawn from, plus 4 hubs at RobotShop EU: EUR 95 + 45 = **EUR 140** incl. VAT. The wheels are on **backorder**.
   - **Alternative:** AliExpress 100 mm sets with 6 mm couplings at EUR 40-115 in listings, about **EUR 25-100 less**. Unverified; the wheel must be 100 x 50 mm, 2 L + 2 R.

**Decided (19 Nov):**
- **No custom main board** (Adam): off-the-shelf modules instead (section "Control and power modules"). Rev B stays parked.
- **Fuse:** one 15 A main fuse on the pack (P5 + P6), as before the board.
- **Sonar dropped:** no HC-SR04 in the design.

## Regenerating

```
python3 cad/v2/scripts/bom.py
```

The script prints the totals and rewrites `BOM.md` and `BOM.csv`. The CSV columns are:
- `price`, `cur`, `incl`: the shown price, its currency, and the VAT rate inside it.
- `vat`: the VAT the buyer pays.
- `unit_eur_net`, `line_eur_net`, `line_eur_gross`: unit and line prices in EUR.
- `total`: `robot` or `optional`.
- `verified`: the date the link was opened.
