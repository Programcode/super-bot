## 18 Nov 2026: Adam's answers on the v2 open points (`cad/v2/`)

| Decision | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Head width stays 190 mm | Adam: he only wanted the neck short, and it is. |
| No generic LED bar; the CPX is the front status light | Adam: "we don't have it". The Circuit Playground Express (~50.6 mm, 10 NeoPixels) sits behind the front wall and shows through the smoked shell, wired to the main board's CPX connector J404 (+5V/GND into its JST battery input, RX, KILL). The bar slot, carrier, diffuser and bezel are removed; J412 LED BAR on the main board stays empty (rev B can drop it). |
| Model only real parts (BOM / decisions), every one wired | Adam's rule. Audit result in `cad/v2/README.md` (Parts audit): the LED bar and a cosmetic "fan hub" disc on the AI HAT+ 2 heatsink came from the photo and are gone; the module parts the main board replaces (DRV8874 perfboard, ESP32 DevKit, PDB + INA226, Pololu bucks, MAX98357A breakout, XT60 loop key) are replaced by the main board block and an XT30 loop key matching its J102. |
| Lidar: both options modelled, switchable with `lidar_rise` | Option A (`lidar_rise` 0): low pod, the 30 mm gimbal knuckle blocks **18.6 deg** (centred 33 deg right of forward), masked in software. Option B (`lidar_rise` 166 mm): a 20 mm mast from the pod, outside the head's sweep, puts the LD19 above the head (scan plane 467 mm): **0 deg** blocked. Renders and a top-down occlusion diagram: `cad/v2/review/`. **Waiting on Adam.** |
| Charging: ideal-diode module in the charge path, jack on the battery door | Jack (5.5 x 2.1, reachable from the back with the shell on) -> reverse-blocking ideal-diode module (LM74700-class, ~20 mV drop so a 12.6 V CC/CV charger still fills the pack; a Schottky's ~0.5 V would stop it near 12.1 V) -> 5 A fuse -> pack (BMS P+). It bypasses the main board, so charging works with the loop key out; the board's INA226 sees discharge only. Consistent with the board input: XT60 -> 15 A fuse -> XT30 loop -> reverse-polarity P-FET. Use a 3S Li-ion 12.6 V 2 A charger. |
| Bumpers: yes, minimal | Reasoning: the lidar plane is 301 mm up (option A) or 467 mm (B) and sees nothing lower; the four cliff sensors look only down; the camera looks only where the head points. That leaves shoes, toy cars, chair bases, cables at wheel height, and the gap between the wheels, plus everything behind or beside the robot when it reverses or strafes. A front bar between the front wheels on 2 microswitches and two rear half bars (either side of the battery door) on 1 switch each, Omron D2F-01L, on the main board's BUMPERS connector J410 (4 inputs). The sides are covered by the wheels themselves (they stick out 56 mm). |
| Main board rev A fits the bay except its two right-angle power connectors | 4.6 mm to each side wall, 2.5 mm over the front motors, the Pi's USB-C over the notch, all signal plugs with room. J101 XT60PW-M and J102 XT30PW-M mate into the right side wall (4.6 mm, ~35 mm needed). Board change: vertical PCB-mount XT60/XT30 at the same spots (recommended) or rotate them to mate across the top edge. Report: `cad/v2/review/main-board-fit.md`. **Waiting on the PCB thread / Adam.** |
